# Agent instructions

## Language

- Use US English spelling in UI text and documentation.
- Preserve official product names, source titles, URLs, and existing internal
  identifiers.

## Calculator URLs and SEO

- Treat `src/routes.json` as the source of truth for calculator paths and
  metadata.
- Preserve the static entry pages and `sitemap.xml` generated by
  `scripts/build-pages.mjs`. The calculators remain client-side applications;
  these entry pages make their content and links available before JavaScript
  loads.
- Preserve migration of legacy `#/calculator?...` links, including their
  configuration. Ordinary pages use path-based URLs and query parameters;
  embedded `?embed=fill` and `?embed=auto` views retain hash navigation.
- Keep configurable query-string variants out of canonical URLs and the
  sitemap.
- The production URL is configured in both `scripts/build-pages.mjs` and
  `src/useHashRoute.ts`; update both if the domain or deployment path changes.
- Deploy all of `dist/`, including calculator directories and `sitemap.xml`, to
  the dedicated calculator directory. Do not overwrite the WordPress root or
  `.htaccess`.
