3. Email sign-in and your maintenance account

When you request a sign-in code, your email address is sent to Supabase Auth. A code is delivered through our configured STRATO SMTP service. Successfully verifying your first code creates an account. An email address is necessary for this sign-in method; without it, you cannot use the cloud Maintenance Log. The other calculators do not require an account.

We process your email address, account identifier, authentication/session information, and the bikes and maintenance history you choose to save. Garage data includes bike names and mileage, components, tasks, service/replacement dates, odometer readings, free-text notes, and synchronization metadata. Please do not enter health information or unnecessary information about other people.

The purpose is to provide your requested account, save your garage, and make it available across devices. Garage data is not published as a public profile. Our authorized administrators and service providers may access data where necessary to operate, support, or secure the service.

5. Optional Intervals.icu connection

You can use the Maintenance Log without Intervals.icu. If you choose to connect, you authorize the connection on Intervals.icu using OAuth with PKCE. The app requests read-only athlete settings access (SETTINGS:READ), not activity or write access. Your browser exchanges a short-lived authorization code directly with Intervals.icu over HTTPS, then sends the resulting access token over HTTPS to our authenticated Supabase connection service. That service requests your athlete profile and gear on your behalf. No client secret or personal API key is requested. Intervals.icu receives your IP address and request information during authorization, and our connection service’s network address and request information during mileage updates. We read the athlete identifier and bike names and mileage; the athlete profile response also contains your name. The imported bike identifiers include the athlete identifier, and the imported bikes and synchronization timestamp are saved in your Supabase garage.

Bike and mileage requests are read-only. Your Intervals.icu connection is saved to your maintenance account, with its OAuth access token encrypted in Supabase Vault. It is separate from your garage JSON and backups. The saved token is accessible only to the authenticated backend service and authorized database administrators; it is not returned to the browser or placed in localStorage or sessionStorage. The browser holds the token transiently during the initial OAuth exchange and secure handoff. Temporary OAuth state and a PKCE verifier are held in tab sessionStorage for the handshake (see below); callback credentials are removed from the page URL.

When you reopen the Maintenance Log or sign in, the saved connection fetches current mileage without another Intervals.icu approval, unless access has expired or been revoked. While connected, refresh runs about every five minutes when the page is visible and no editing form is open. There is no background scheduler while the page is closed. Signing out or leaving the page keeps the server-side connection for your next sign-in. “Disconnect Intervals.icu” erases the saved credential and attempts to revoke the grant. Account deletion also removes the connection and encrypted secret. If provider revocation is unavailable, revoke this app in Intervals.icu Settings → Apps. Disconnecting does not delete previously imported bikes or maintenance records; these can be managed in your garage.

We process this information to provide the optional synchronization you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Intervals.icu is operated by Intervals.icu Ltd., 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. It acts as a separate controller for its own service, not as the processor hosting your maintenance garage. Its handling of your account is described in its privacy policy.

According to that policy (effective November 10, 2026), Intervals.icu processes account, activity, and wellness data in Germany and Finland, with cloud storage in EU regions using Google, Backblaze, Wasabi, and Scaleway. It reports error/crash processing by Sentry in Germany and also lists other providers for its own analytics, notifications, and payments. These describe Intervals.icu's service; our calculator does not embed those analytics, payment, or notification services merely by connecting to its API.

You can delete your separate Intervals.icu account under “Settings → Delete Account” in its website or apps. Its policy states that deleted data is removed from its backups within 30 days, subject to legally required retention such as purchase invoices. This is Intervals.icu's retention statement, not a backup-deletion deadline for our Supabase garage. For requests concerning Intervals.icu, its policy also provides a privacy representative portal.

You can delete individual bikes and entries, delete your account using “More → Delete account” when signed in, or request deletion using the contact above. We do not currently maintain separate operator-managed logs or server backups. This does not establish whether our hosting, authentication, database, and email providers retain their own operational logs or backup copies. Garage backups you choose to download remain under your control.

Account deletion must include the associated garage and relevant processor-held data, subject to justified retention obligations. If providers retain backup copies, document when they expire and ensure deleted data is not reintroduced into active service after restoration.
